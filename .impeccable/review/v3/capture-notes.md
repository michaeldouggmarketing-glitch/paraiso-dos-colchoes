# V3 capture evidence

The normal-motion first viewport captures cover 1440×1000, 1280×800, 390×844 and 360×800. The two Castor scene captures show actual normal-motion scrolling to the third model. Four-size behavioral verification is recorded in verification.json.

The headless Chromium fullPage compositor repeated earlier sections within a long screenshot. Those malformed captures were discarded. desktop.png and mobile.png were rebuilt from fresh, actual viewport screenshots in reduced motion, beginning at the document top and continuing to the footer. capture-manifest.json records each tile's actual scrollY, viewport size, document height and section rectangles. The persistent WhatsApp control therefore repeats at tile boundaries in this overview; section and hero screenshots show its actual position. Page content and layout were not altered for the capture.

All full-page and section captures were opened and validated before sending for finish review. Reduced motion intentionally removes scroll pinning and entrance effects, providing a complete static document. Normal-motion hero/Castor captures and motion.js provide separate evidence for the coordinated scene implementation.
